Notice · the one interview
Talk to the person it happens to
Not your mother or your father. Someone who lives the problem. You are not there to tell them your idea. You are there to find out you were wrong about something.
My grandmother, Umm Khalid
Takes four different tablets every day. Talked to her on Friday after lunch, 20 minutes.
Recorded
Question 1
Show me the last time this happened.
She took me to the kitchen and opened the drawer. She keeps them in a drawer, not the cupboard, because the cupboard is too high for her now.
Question 2
What do you do about it now?
She scratches the lid of one box with a knife so she can feel which is which without her glasses. She has done this for years and never told anyone.
Question 3
What would you never give up?
She does not want anything that beeps or reminds her. She said it would make her feel like she is being watched in her own house.
What I got wrong
I thought she could not read the labels. She reads them fine. The problem is she takes them in the dark at 5am before prayer and does not turn the light on.
